Kseniia Panferova

Architect · Landscape Designer

Belgrade, Serbia

Profile

Architect based in Belgrade, working across landscape architecture, public space, mapping and spatial research. My current practice combines concept development for public parks in Abu Dhabi with research methods including GIS, cartography, field observation and analytical drawing. I am particularly interested in design processes that begin with a careful reading of territory and translate complex spatial, ecological and social conditions into clear strategies.

Selected Projects & Research

Borderless — Ada Zoo, Belgrade

Competition proposal with Polina Minina · 2025

Co-developed the spatial concept and zoning strategy for the relocation of Belgrade Zoo, exploring ways of reducing the visual presence of enclosures and creating the perception of continuous animal habitats. Exhibited at the 48th Salon of Architecture, Museum of Applied Art, Belgrade, 2026.

A City Without Borders: Administrative Boundaries and Spatial Identity in Belgrade

Master’s thesis, University of Belgrade · 2025

Investigated the relationship between administrative boundaries, built fabric and lived urban conditions in Belgrade. Combined GIS, demographic and spatial data, satellite imagery and field observation to examine spatial differences obscured by aggregated administrative statistics.

Last Glow — Mapping Italian Coastal Watchtowers

Reuse Italy competition · 2023

Created and geolocated a dataset of approximately 180 coastal watchtowers and developed a Google Maps–QGIS workflow to analyse their territorial distribution as part of the design research.

Contemporary Tombstones: Prototypes and Modes of Interpretation

Moscow Architectural Institute · 2022

Field-based study of funerary architecture in Moscow and St Petersburg. Developed a typology of recurring architectural prototypes and analysed their transformations in contemporary memorial forms.

Reading the Urban Edge through Walking and Photography

Belgrade · 2026–ongoing

Independent photographic fieldwork exploring urban peripheries, terminal bus stops and transitional landscapes through walking, route documentation and field notes.
